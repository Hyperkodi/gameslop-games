THE MISSION DOSSIER

An automated army has taken over the island. You're a sentient blob with a very large gun. Work with what you've got.

    Easy: 12 lives, frequent guns, two weapon slots. Normal: 3 lives, fewer guns, two slots. Hard: 3 lives, more enemies, rare guns, one slot, no nukes. A duplicate of an equipped or holstered gun upgrades it to tier 5. Easy and Normal remember upgrades for the run; Hard loses them when a gun is discarded. V / J / gamepad Y / SWAP switches slots. Cloak breaks enemy tracking for 8 seconds; bullets and contact remain dangerous. Nukes clear visible non-boss enemies and enemy shots. All modes include three continues.
