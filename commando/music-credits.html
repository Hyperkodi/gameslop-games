Music & Credits

Eight quieter stage themes. These players loop and use the game's background music volume. Playing a new track stops the previous one.

All tracks are available under CC0 1.0 / public domain. We chose the CC0 option where multiple licenses are listed. Credits are included in appreciation of the artists.

Game versions have adjusted loudness, short loop blends and Ogg Vorbis encoding. The original recordings are linked below.
