This player uses EmulatorJS 4.2.3, distributed under the GNU GPL v3, from its versioned CDN.

The default N64 core is Mupen64Plus-Next. ParaLLEl N64 is available with ?core=parallel. Core notices are available in the emulator information menu and upstream repositories.

The native asset format work references mkst's Conker decompilation and the GLideN64 CBFD display-list documentation and implementation.
